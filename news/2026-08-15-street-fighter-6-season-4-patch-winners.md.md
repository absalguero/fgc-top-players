Knockdown projectiles, restored frame advantage, and expanded neutral tools have vaulted Luke back to the forefront of competitive play in the Street Fighter 6 Season 4 balance update.

<div class="takeaways">

**Key Takeaways:**
* **Luke Reclaims Dominant Neutral:** Sand Blaster counter-hits now grant hard knockdowns into drive rush pressure, while crouching medium punch regains three active frames.
* **Low-Tier Upgrades:** Jamie receives extended range on standing heavy kick and safe Rekka pressure, while Alex gains complete projectile invincibility on DDT.
* **Expanded Utility Across the Cast:** Kimberly gains three spray cans with separate reload inputs, and Cammy secures a plus-five drive rush shimmy normal.

## Luke and Key Specialists Lead Season 4 Overhaul

Tournament brackets are set for a major shakeup following an extensive balance sweep across the roster. In a comprehensive patch breakdown, FGC content creator VesperArcade highlighted Luke as the primary beneficiary of the update. The character receives direct reversions and enhancements to the toolkit that defined his initial tournament dominance. Light Sand Blaster features extended travel distance, complementing projectile knockdowns on counter-hit and punish counter. That adjustment provides immediate okizeme opportunities across full-screen interactions.

Luke also regains his signature button presence. Crouching medium punch now stays active for three frames instead of two, restoring consistent drive rush checks and meaty links into crouching heavy punch. Standing heavy punch sits at minus three on block, turning heavy punch into medium Sand Blaster into an airtight sequence that eliminates perfect parry gaps. With level two Super damage boosted and dragon punches hitting harder, top competitors like Kawano, Chris Wong, and MenaRD are already evaluating roster switches back to the character.

Specialist picks also received structural upgrades in the analysis. Kimberly players now manage up to three spray cans, supported by a dedicated down-down kick reload input that prevents accidental item deployment. Her sprint now includes a hold-to-feint option for deceptive spacing, while standing medium kick functions as a reliable, cancelable poke.

## Neutral Reworks Shape the Competitive Horizon

The Season 4 patch emphasizes active neutral engagement over passive defense. Jamie gains expanded whiff-punish potential through an elongated standing heavy kick that pushes opponents backward on block while pulling his own hurtbox away from low counter-pokes. His OD Rekka is safe at minus three, allowing continuous block pressure into burnout situations.

Grapplers receive tactical anti-zoning remedies. Alex bypasses fireball traps entirely thanks to invincibility on Angery DDT, turning predictable projectile patterns into high-damage command slam opportunities. Cammy players gain delayed light attack timing to trap delayed tech responses, alongside a plus-five back medium punch out of drive rush that sets up walk-back shimmies without requiring defensive backdashes. As players digest these system adjustments, tournament character diversity is positioned to shift throughout upcoming Premier qualifiers.
