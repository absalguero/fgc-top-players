<div class="takeaways">

**Key Takeaways:**
* **Ryu Ascends to S-Tier:** Ryu joins Terry, Blanka, JP, Mai, and Sagat at the highest rank, while Ed falls to B-tier.
* **Ken and Chun-Li Drop to C-Tier:** Longtime tournament favorites Ken and Chun-Li slide from B-tier into C-tier alongside Alex, Dhalsim, and Juri.
* **Yasmine Lands in D-Tier:** The newest roster addition begins her competitive journey in D-tier alongside Ingrid, E. Honda, and Marisa.

---

## Ryu Ascends while Ed and Legacy Staples Tumble

French competitor Kilzyou reshuffled the competitive landscape in an evaluation published on August 26, 2026. His post-patch SF6 tier list challenges several long-held community assumptions, rewarding explosive neutral play while penalizing former tournament dominators.

Ryu makes the leap to join Terry, Blanka, JP, Mai, and Sagat in S-tier. That promotion gives Ryu the edge over every other shoto on the roster. Ed drops down to B-tier, signaling a sharp decline in his perceived tournament dominance.

Luke climbs out of B-tier to join A-tier, standing alongside proven threats like Rashid, Guile, C. Viper, Cammy, and Akuma. Both Zangief and Elena retain their strong status within A-tier.

The lower tiers show intense turbulence. Jamie, Lily, and Manon climb up from C-tier into B-tier, finding better positioning against the reshuffled field. Conversely, Ken, Chun-Li, and Alex fall into C-tier with Juri and Dhalsim. Ingrid and Marisa slip down into D-tier, where newcomer Yasmine also lands next to E. Honda.

---

## Competitive Repercussions for Upcoming Tournaments

These rankings point toward a noticeable shift in tournament preparation. Competitors accustomed to facing endless Ken and Chun-Li brackets may need to redirect practice time toward neutral-heavy matchups against Ryu, Terry, and Sagat.

If these placements hold true across the Premier circuit, character diversity in Top 8s could look completely refreshed over the coming months.
