Two weeks before Street Fighter League begins, ZETA DIVISION esports competitors MenaRD, Momochi, and Yamaguchi placed Ryu and Terry at the top of their Street Fighter 6 tier list while sending Akuma straight to B-tier.

<div class="takeaways">

**Key Takeaways:**
* **Shoto Dominance:** Ryu and Terry anchor S-tier, establishing a new competitive baseline.
* **Former Giants Fall:** Former top-tier staples Akuma and JP dropped to lower tiers due to unfavorable matchups against top shotos.
* **SFL Readiness:** The ZETA DIVISION roster evaluated character performance across current balance updates to prepare for team league play.

</div>

## Shoto Dominance Leads S-Tier Selection

During the ZETA STREET LIVE #22 broadcast, the ZETA DIVISION squad gathered to evaluate the current Street Fighter 6 competitive landscape. With team member Higuchi away at CEO, Momochi, Yamaguchi, MenaRD, and JacK took on the task of building a consensus ranking.

The crew locked in Ryu as a unanimous S-tier character. MenaRD went a step further, highlighting Terry right alongside Ryu as the strongest option in the game. Guile, Luke, Dee Jay, Elena, C. Viper, and Mai joined Ryu and Terry in S-tier, creating a group heavy on fireballs, neutral control, and space control.

> **"Ryu is 100% S, but not the best. I think Terry may be the best."**
> — *MenaRD, ZETA DIVISION Player*

---

## Major Meta Shifts: Akuma and JP Fall to Lower Tiers

The biggest discussion centered around characters dropping in effective tournament strength. MenaRD and the team pushed Akuma down to B-tier, pointing to struggles against the refreshed shoto archetype and strong neutral fighters. JP suffered a similar drop, landing in lower A-tier as top-tier characters maintain tools that bypass his zoning pressure. E. Honda sat alone at the bottom in D-tier.
