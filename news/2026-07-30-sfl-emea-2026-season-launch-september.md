Street Fighter League Europe just absorbed an entire region, and the six rosters set to fight it out from September 2 through 6 tell the story better than any press release could.

<div class="takeaways">

**Key Takeaways:**
* **The newly merged Street Fighter League EMEA Asia begins its first season on September 2, running through September 6.**
* **Six teams are competing, blending longtime European talent with players pulled from Asia and the SFL USA and SFL Japan circuits.**
* **Episodes stream live daily starting at 4PM CEST on Capcom Fighters.**

</div>

---

## A Merged League, Not a New One

Capcom had originally planned a standalone Asian edition of the Street Fighter League before folding those plans into the existing European league instead, creating Street Fighter League EMEA Asia in the process. The result is a roster pool that mixes players who built their names across separate regional circuits into a single six-team season.

Episodes go live daily at 4PM CEST starting September 2, running through September 6.

Several names carry storylines into the new format. Three of Team Falcons' four players previously competed together as Kuaishou Gaming in Street Fighter League USA, with Kusanagi the lone new face. NL, DCQ, and Leshar are all making their EMEA Asia debuts after coming up through SFL USA and SFL Japan.

Red Dunes Esports, the new organization co-owned by AngryBird and Big Bird, carries over much of the roster that won the title under Ninjas in Pyjamas, an organization that will not return for this season.

---

## Road to the Finals

With six teams locked in and one roster still pending, the opening week of EMEA Asia becomes the first real test of how these regional talent pools mesh under one banner. Further details on GTW's lineup and the match schedule are expected as September 2 approaches.
