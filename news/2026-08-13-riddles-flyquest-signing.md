FlyQuest has locked down Riddles as their cornerstone Street Fighter 6 player following his standout performances with Terry Bogard across global majors.

<div class="takeaways">

**Key Takeaways:**
* **Full-Time Roster Addition:** Riddles joins FlyQuest under the organization's FlyFighters banner after serving as a key competitor in Street Fighter League.
* **Terry Specialist:** Known for high-execution offense, Riddles has established himself as one of the premier Terry Bogard players worldwide.
* **Roster Reinforcement:** The signing secures FlyQuest a proven open-bracket contender capable of making deep runs at major tournaments.

</div>

---

## From Draft Pick to Main Roster Anchor

FlyQuest announced the signing on August 13, 2026, bringing Riddles into the organization's core fighting game division. The move formalizes a relationship that built momentum over the past year through team league competition and open tournament brackets.

Riddles previously represented the organization as a draft player during Street Fighter League Pro-US. His high-impact performances in clutch team scenarios proved his ability to deliver under pressure. By securing him on a full contract, FlyQuest establishes a clear long-term commitment to maintaining a top-level presence in Street Fighter 6.

---

## Heavyweight Results in Street Fighter 6

After initial success in the early days of Street Fighter 6 using Luke, Riddles shifted his focus completely upon the release of Terry Bogard. That character transition elevated his game on the international stage. His aggressive, resource-heavy playstyle quickly established his Terry as one of the most dangerous picks in the current meta.

His tournament record reflects that growth. Riddles turned heads with a fourth-place finish at Ultimate Fighting Arena 2025 before carrying that momentum into the 2026 season. At Blink Respawn 2026, he fought through a stacked field featuring international talent to earn a Top 8 spot. He followed that performance at Evo 2026 by placing 25th in a bracket of thousands, notably eliminating Street Fighter legend Daigo Umehara along the way.

---

## Reinforcing the FlyFighters Division

The acquisition gives FlyQuest a high-caliber open-bracket threat for the remainder of the Capcom Pro Tour season. With high-level tournament experience and a proven track record against world-class opponents, Riddles brings both consistency and peak explosive potential to the roster.

FlyQuest now turns its focus to upcoming premier events, with Riddles leading the charge for the team in Capcom Pro Tour qualifiers and open brackets worldwide.
